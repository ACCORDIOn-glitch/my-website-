How ordering works

There are three ways to order, and all three end with a receipt on your email.

Method 01

Shop online and send the cart

Add items in the sizes you want, send the order, and we will email your account details and a receipt. Pay by bank transfer and we start packing.

Method 02

Do not know what to buy

Tell us who the food is for and your budget on the request page. We build the basket, call you to agree it, and only take money once you are happy.

Method 03

Transfer first, talk later

If you already know what you want, transfer to the account on the home page and press "I have sent the money". We confirm receipt and get packing.

Delivery

We deliver nationwide. Same-day within Lagos for orders placed before 2pm, next-day for major cities, and two to three working days elsewhere.

Frozen goods travel in cold-boxes so they arrive safe. Free delivery on orders over the threshold shown at checkout.

Payments

We take bank transfer, which is the most reliable option for both of us. You will receive our account details with your receipt, and a confirmation as soon as the money lands.

If you are buying for a shop or a large order, ask for the wholesale rate on the request page and we will price it for you before you pay anything.
